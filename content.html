CHICHEN ITZA

Chichen Itza is one of the most famous ancient cities of the Maya civilization in Mexico. It is known for its impressive pyramid called El Castillo, which was built to honor the Maya god Kukulkan. Chichen Itza was an important center for religion, trade, and culture.
Today, it is a popular tourist attraction and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Chichen Itza is a beautiful symbol of the rich history, culture, and achievements of the ancient Maya civilization.

MACHU PICCHU

Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca citadel located high in the Andes Mountains of Peru. Built in the 15th century, it is famous for its impressive stone buildings, terraces, and breathtaking mountain scenery. The site is believed to have been an important religious, cultural, and royal center of the Inca civilization. Today, Machu Picchu is one of the world’s most famous archaeological sites and attracts visitors from around the globe.
